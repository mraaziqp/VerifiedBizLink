## IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

### "Page didn't load"
**Say**: "Let me refresh that." (Press F5)
**Don't say**: "Sorry, this doesn't usually happen"

### "Button didn't work"
**Say**: "Let me try that again." (Click again)
**If it fails again**: "We're on a dev server - let me click the back button and try the other route."

### "They ask 'When can we launch?'"
**Say**: "We can deploy to production in 1-2 weeks after sign-off. We need to set up the production database, email service, and train your admin team."

### "They ask 'Can you show me X?'"
**Say**: "I can show you that in the [next section / in the admin panel / on mobile]. Let me get there..."
