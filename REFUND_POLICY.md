# Refund Policy

**Last Updated: July 2026**

## 1. Overview

VerifiedBizLink is committed to providing quality business verification services. This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which refunds may be requested and issued.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 Eligible for Refund
- Services not rendered as described
- Technical failures preventing service access
- Duplicate charges
- Unauthorized transactions
- Services cancelled within 24 hours of purchase

### 2.2 Not Eligible for Refund
- Services successfully completed and delivered
- Verification rejections due to incomplete or inaccurate documentation
- Change of mind after service delivery
- Non-payment of associated fees
- Services used beyond the refund window

## 3. Refund Request Process

### 3.1 Submitting a Request
Refund requests must be submitted within **30 days** of the transaction date. Requests received after this period will not be considered.

To request a refund, contact our support team at [support email] with:
- Transaction ID
- Date of transaction
- Detailed reason for refund request
- Supporting documentation (if applicable)

### 3.2 Review Period
- Initial review: 5-7 business days
- We will communicate our decision via email
- Appeals can be submitted within 10 business days of our decision

## 4. Refund Processing

### 4.1 Approval & Processing Time
- Approved refunds are processed within 5-10 business days
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- Bank processing times may vary (typically 3-5 business days)

### 4.2 Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued where:
- Only a portion of services were affected
- Partial services were successfully rendered
- Administrative fees apply to service cancellation

## 5. Special Circumstances

### 5.1 Verification Rejection
- If a business verification is rejected, no refund is issued
- The service fee covers the verification attempt
- Resubmission may be available at reduced cost

### 5.2 Disputes
For disputed transactions or payment fraud claims:
1. Contact support immediately with evidence
2. We will investigate within 10 business days
3. Disputes may be escalated to your payment provider

## 6. Cancellation Policy

### 6.1 Service Cancellation
- Services may be cancelled prior to completion
- Cancellations within 24 hours receive a full refund
- Cancellations after 24 hours are subject to applicable fees

### 6.2 Subscription Services
- Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled anytime
- No refund for the current billing period
- Future billing will cease immediately

## 7. Non-Refundable Items

The following are strictly non-refundable:
- Verification attempts completed successfully
- Administrative and processing fees
- Late payment penalties
- Services rendered as requested
- Digital products delivered and accessed

## 8. Contact Information

For refund inquiries or disputes:
- **Email:** support@verifiedbizlink.co.za
- **Business Hours:** Monday - Friday, 08:00 - 17:00 SAST
- **Response Time:** Within 24 business hours

## 9. Changes to This Policy

VerifiedBizLink reserves the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting to our website. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 10. Governing Law

This Refund Policy is governed by the laws of the Republic of South Africa and subject to applicable consumer protection legislation.
