## 📋 What Each Feature Does (For Reference)

### Home Feed
- Users see posts from verified businesses
- Can create their own posts
- Can like and comment on posts
- Can search by name/company
- Can filter by industry
- Compliance news widget shows regulatory updates

### Business Verification (Vetting Hub)
- Business uploads 5 required documents:
  1. CIPC Registration Certificate
  2. VAT Compliance Letter
  3. Identity Proof of Directors
  4. Bank Proof (Bank Letter or Statement)
  5. Proof Business Exists
- Status changes: Pending → Reviewing → Verified
- Admin reviews and approves
- Verified badge (gold checkmark) appears

### Networking
- Browse verified businesses
- Send connection requests
- Accept/reject requests
- View all connections
- Search and filter connections
- View connection profiles

### Admin Panel
- Live dashboard with stats
- Vetting Desk shows pending businesses
- Review documents
- Approve or reject with feedback
- View user management
- See audit logs of all actions
- Generate reports

### Settings
- Edit profile (name, headline, location, bio, phone, avatar)
- Change password
- Notification preferences
- Privacy settings
- Delete account
