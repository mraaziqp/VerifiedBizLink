## 📊 Platform Overview - 5 Core Pillars

### 1. **Verification-First Architecture**
- **What it does**: Every business goes through structured verification before they can participate
- **How it works**: 
  - Business uploads 5 required documents: CIPC cert, VAT compliance letter, ID proof, bank letter, business proof
  - Admin team reviews each submission in the Vetting Desk
  - Status changes: Pending → Reviewing → Verified (or Rejected with feedback)
  - Verified badge appears on profile (Gold checkmark + 95 trust score)
- **Why it matters**: Removes the "is this company real?" question upfront
- **Demo moment**: Show vetting flow → verification status → beautiful verified badge

### 2. **B2B Networking with Trust Built In**
- **What it does**: Connect with verified businesses in your industry
- **How it works**:
  - Browse featured verified businesses with trust scores and reviews
  - Send connection requests (requires both parties to be verified)
  - Accept/reject connections on Network page
  - See all your connections with search/filter
- **Why it matters**: Every connection is with a verified, real business
- **Demo moment**: Browse businesses → send connection → show Network tab with acceptance flow

### 3. **Activity Feed & Industry Insights**
- **What it does**: Stay updated on your network and industry compliance trends
- **How it works**:
  - Create posts to share insights, ask questions, announce updates
  - Like and comment on posts from your network
  - Auto-categorize by industry (Manufacturing, Services, Finance, etc.)
  - Compliance news widget shows regulatory updates
- **Why it matters**: Turns network into an intelligence source
- **Demo moment**: Post something → show likes/comments → show compliance news widget

### 4. **Business Profiles with Reviews & Ratings**
- **What it does**: Build social proof and credibility
- **How it works**:
  - Every business has a structured profile (company name, industry, website, phone, address)
  - Connections can leave verified reviews (5-star rating + comments)
  - Reviews are tagged by industry category
  - Helpful votes show which reviews matter most
- **Why it matters**: Reviews from verified businesses are actually meaningful
- **Demo moment**: Open a business profile → show reviews → explain trust score calculation

### 5. **Admin Verification Desk**
- **What it does**: Streamlined dashboard for reviewing business submissions
- **How it works**:
  - See all pending/reviewing businesses with document status
  - Review documents (view/download PDFs)
  - Approve or reject with custom feedback
  - Real-time stats on verification rate, bottlenecks
- **Why it matters**: Fast admin workflow = faster business onboarding
- **Demo moment**: Show admin panel → browse pending businesses → show stats dashboard
