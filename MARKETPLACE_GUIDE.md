## 🎨 Features & Tabs

### **Tab 1: Prices** 📊
Browse and analyze commodity prices.

#### Search
- Find commodities by name or symbol
- Real-time search as you type
- Example: Search "gold" → shows Gold, Silver, Platinum

#### Filter by Category
- **Precious Metals** → Gold, Silver, Platinum, Palladium
- **Industrial Metals** → Copper, Lithium
- **Energy** → Oil, Gas, Uranium
- **Agriculture** → Wheat, Corn, Soybeans

#### Price Cards
Each commodity card shows:
- 📈 **Price** - Current market price
- 📊 **24h Change** - Dollar amount + percentage
- 🔼/🔽 **Trend** - Up (green) / Down (red)
- 📍 **24h Range** - High and low prices
- ❤️ **Watchlist** - Click heart to add/remove

#### 24-Hour Charts
1. **Click any commodity card** to view detailed chart
2. **Chart displays:** Historical prices for last 24 hours
3. **Chart color:** Green (up trend) / Red (down trend)
4. **Close chart:** Click "Close" button to return to list

---

### **Tab 2: Market News** 📰
Stay updated on market movements and events.

#### News Items Include
- 📰 **Title** - Clear headline
- 📄 **Summary** - 2-3 sentence summary
- 🏷️ **Impact** - Positive / Negative / Neutral / Mixed
- ⭐ **Importance** - High / Medium / Low
- 🔗 **Related Commodities** - Which commodities are affected
- 👤 **Source** - Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, etc.
- 🕐 **Time** - How long ago published

#### Example News Items
- "Gold Prices Hit 6-Month High" (Positive impact on GOLD)
- "Copper Demand Surges" (Positive impact on COPPER)
- "Oil Markets Stable" (Neutral impact on WTICL, BRENL)
- "Lithium Prices Decline" (Negative impact on LITHIUM)

#### Using News
- **Track impacts:** See which commodities are trending
- **Make decisions:** Read full summaries before trading
- **Prioritize:** High importance items first
- **Cross-reference:** Check related commodities for correlation

---

### **Tab 3: Watchlist** 💖
Your personal commodity tracking list.

#### How to Use
1. **Add to watchlist:** Click heart ❤️ on any commodity
2. **View your list:** Go to Watchlist tab
3. **Remove:** Click heart again to remove
4. **Track together:** Compare multiple commodities side-by-side

#### Why Use Watchlist?
- ✅ Focus on commodities you care about

## 📊 Understanding Price Data

### **Price Change Indicators**
```
+12.30 (+0.47%)     ← Gold went up $12.30 or 0.47%
-15.40 (-1.45%)     ← Platinum went down $15.40 or 1.45%
```

### **24-Hour Range**
```
High: $2,655.80     ← Highest price in last 24 hours
Low:  $2,630.20     ← Lowest price in last 24 hours
Current: $2,645.50  ← Right now
```

### **Trend Colors**
- 🟢 **Green (Up)** - Price trending upward, buy interest increasing
- 🔴 **Red (Down)** - Price trending downward, sell pressure increasing

## 🔔 News Impact Meanings

### **Impact Levels**
- 🟢 **Positive** - Good news, bullish outlook, buy signals
- 🔴 **Negative** - Bad news, bearish outlook, sell signals
- 🟡 **Neutral** - News that doesn't strongly favor either direction
- 🔵 **Mixed** - Both positive and negative aspects

### **Importance Levels**
- 🔴 **High** - Major market move, must-read, affects multiple commodities
- 🟡 **Medium** - Moderate importance, impacts specific sectors
- ⚪ **Low** - Minor news, background information

## 🛠️ Technical Details

### **API Endpoints**

#### Get Commodity Prices
```bash
GET /api/market/prices
GET /api/market/prices?category=Precious Metals
GET /api/market/prices?symbol=GOLD
```

Response includes: price, change, 24h high/low, trend, category

#### Get Market News
```bash
GET /api/market/news
GET /api/market/news?limit=10
GET /api/market/news?importance=high
GET /api/market/news?category=Precious Metals
```

Response includes: title, summary, impact, source, related commodities

### **Components**
- `PriceCard` - Display individual commodity prices
- `MarketNewsCard` - Display news with tags
- `PriceChart` - 24-hour price trend visualization
