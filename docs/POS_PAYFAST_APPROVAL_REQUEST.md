Hello,

We run VerifiedBizLink (verifiedbizlink.co.za), a South African platform that verifies small businesses. We already use PayFast for our own subscriptions (merchant ID [YOUR MERCHANT ID]).

We have added a point-of-sale (till) product for small traders such as market stalls and spaza shops. Today, those merchants are paid by their customers in cash, or on their own card machines, so the money never passes through us. They pay us a monthly subscription and a 1% platform fee per sale, both billed through our existing PayFast account. We are not asking about that.

We would like to let merchants accept card and Instant EFT payments from their customers through a PayFast payment link or QR code shown on the till. Before we switch anything on, we need your guidance and written approval on how this can be done correctly.

1. **Collecting on behalf of merchants.** May we take payments for goods sold by independent merchants into our own PayFast account and pay those merchants out ourselves? If not, what arrangement do you require?
2. **Split payments.** Can your split-payment feature support each merchant having their own PayFast account and receiving the sale, while we receive a percentage (for example 1%) as a platform fee? What are the onboarding and KYC requirements for each merchant?
3. **Merchant of record.** In each model, who is the merchant of record? Who handles refunds, chargebacks and disputes?
4. **Settlement.** How and when are merchants paid out? Can the platform fee be deducted automatically?
5. **Notifications.** Do ITNs for merchants' own accounts reach our notify URL, and how should we verify them?
6. **Commercial terms.** What are the fees for the arrangement you recommend, and are there volume or category restrictions? Our merchants are mainly informal and small retail businesses.

We will only enable customer payments through PayFast after we receive your written confirmation of the approved model.
