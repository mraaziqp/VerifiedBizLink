# VerifiedBizLink POS: merchant terms (draft for legal review)

> **This is a working draft for a South African attorney to review and complete. It is not legal advice and must not be published as is.** It describes how the system actually behaves, so the lawyer can turn that into binding terms. Items marked **[LAWYER]** need a legal decision.

## 1. What the service is
VerifiedBizLink POS is software for recording sales, stock, purchases and expenses. **VerifiedBizLink does not receive, hold or pay out the money your customers pay you.** Customers pay you directly, in cash or on your own card machine.

## 2. Fees
1. **Setup fee:** a once-off fee (currently R50), charged with your first month.
2. **Subscription:** a monthly fee (currently R99 on the Starter plan), billed by PayFast each month until you cancel.
3. **Transaction fee:** a percentage of each completed sale (currently 1%). The rate that applies is shown on the Payments & Fees page and stored with each sale.
4. **Refunds:** if you refund a sale, the fee on the refunded part is given back to you.
5. **Monthly statements:** on the first day of each month you receive a statement of the previous month's transaction fees, less fees given back for refunds, plus any balance or credit carried forward. It is payable within 7 days through PayFast. Amounts under R5 are carried to the next statement.
6. **Price changes:** we may change prices on [LAWYER: notice period] days' notice. A new transaction fee applies only to sales made after it takes effect. Your subscription price stays at the amount you signed up at unless you change plan.
7. **VAT:** [LAWYER: are VerifiedBizLink's fees VAT-inclusive, and is VerifiedBizLink a VAT vendor?]

## 3. Late payment
If a subscription payment or a statement is late, your till keeps working for a 5-day grace period. After that it becomes read-only: you cannot record new sales, but you can still see and export all your records. Paying restores full access. **We never delete your records because of late payment.**

## 4. Cancellation
You can cancel any time from Payments & Fees. Billing stops, and your till works until the end of the month you have paid for. Transaction fees already earned are still billed. ## 5. Your data
1. Your business records belong to you. You can export your products, sales, refunds, purchases, stock history, ledger, statements and expenses as CSV at any time, even while your account is read-only.
2. VerifiedBizLink staff can view merchant POS records only for support, billing and fraud prevention. Every view is logged.

## 6. Your customers' information (POPIA)
If you record customers' names, phone numbers or email addresses (for example, to send a WhatsApp receipt), you are the responsible party for that information and VerifiedBizLink is your operator. ## 7. Your responsibilities
1. Your prices are correct and your goods are lawful.
2. **VAT:** you charge and declare VAT only if you are VAT-registered. The till shows VAT only when you have entered a valid VAT number.
3. **Card sales:** you confirm a card sale only after your own card machine has approved it.
4. **Staff:** you are responsible for the staff you give access to, and for keeping PINs private.

## 8. Payment methods not yet offered
Tap-on-phone card payments, and QR or payment-link sales collected through VerifiedBizLink, are not available. They will only be offered once approved by the payment provider and covered by updated terms.
